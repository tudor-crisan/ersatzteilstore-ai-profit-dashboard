### Challenge Task Description

**Project:** AI Profit Dashboard (E-Commerce Unit Economics & Intelligent Control System)

Intelligently connects all relevant data sources for an e-commerce store:
- **Shopify:** Sales, orders, products, margins, returns, COGS, payment fees, shipping costs.
- **Google Ads & Merchant Center:** Ad performance, product feeds, CPC, ROAS, POAS.
- **Meta Business Manager & TikTok Ads:** Ad spend, blended CAC, campaign performance.
- **Google Analytics 4:** Traffic channels, conversion rates, customer journey & behavior.

### Core Objectives
1. **True Profit Transparency (Beyond vanity metrics):**
   - Not just a standard reporting dashboard.
   - Calculates Net Profit (Contribution Margin 1, 2, 3) = Revenue - COGS - Ad Spend - Shipping - Returns - Payment Processing.
   - POAS (Profit on Ad Spend) and true product-level margin.

2. **Conversational AI Q&A Engine:**
   - Natural language queries:
     - *"Which products are the most profitable after deducting all costs?"*
     - *"Where are we currently losing money?"* (Bleeding ad campaigns, high-return SKUs, negative margin sales).
     - *"Which marketing budgets should we optimize?"* (Reallocation suggestions from unprofitable ad sets to winning product clusters).

3. **Proactive AI Monitoring & Autonomous Recommendations:**
   - AI continuously monitors metrics and flags anomalies / leakages.
   - Actionable one-click recommendations (e.g., "Pause Ad Set X - loss of 450 CHF over 7 days", "Raise budget on High-Margin SKU Y in DE region").

4. **Architecture & PoC Scope:**
   - Start with core channels: **Shopify + Google Ads + Meta Ads**.
   - Modular, extensible architecture ready to scale to TikTok Ads, GA4, Merchant Center, and multi-country expansion (Switzerland CHF, Germany EUR, Spain EUR, Italy EUR).
